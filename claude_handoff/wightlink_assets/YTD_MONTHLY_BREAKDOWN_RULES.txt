YTD Monthly Breakdown Rules

Goal
Add extra YTD slides for selected campaign types/channels without replacing the existing quarterly slides.

New slides
- Brand Monthly Breakdown YTD
- Generics Monthly Breakdown YTD
- PMax Performance Summary YTD

YTD definition
YTD means first month of the report year through the last month of the report quarter.
For Q2 2026:
- Jan 2026
- Feb 2026
- Mar 2026
- Apr 2026
- May 2026
- Jun 2026

Source
Use the performance CSV with daily rows. Example supplied:
sample_inputs_ytd_plan/performance_daily_over_year_sample.csv

Required filtering
- Brand Monthly Breakdown YTD: Campaign Type = Brand
- Generics Monthly Breakdown YTD: Campaign Type = Generic or Generics, depending on source naming
- PMax Performance Summary YTD: Campaign Type = PMax, Performance Max, or equivalent source naming

Monthly aggregation
For each month, calculate:
- Cost = sum Cost
- Purchases = sum Purchases
- Purchase Revenue = sum Purchase Revenue
- CPA = Cost / Purchases
- ROAS = Purchase Revenue / Cost
- AOV = Purchase Revenue / Purchases
- Impressions = sum Impressions if shown
- Clicks = sum Clicks if shown
- CTR = Clicks / Impressions if shown
- CPC = Cost / Clicks if shown

Table format
Use the same visual style as the reference monthly breakdown table:
- dark header row
- white/very light body rows
- total row at bottom
- red Total label
- currency and numeric formatting matching the deck

Required report sections
Add:
- [Brand Monthly Breakdown YTD]
- [Generics Monthly Breakdown YTD]
- [PMax Performance Summary YTD]

Placement
Add each YTD slide immediately after the matching existing quarterly slide:
- Brand Monthly Breakdown YTD after Brand Monthly Breakdown
- Generics Monthly Breakdown YTD after Generics Monthly Breakdown
- PMax Performance Summary YTD after PMax Performance Summary

Bullets
Generate 1-3 concise bullets based on the YTD monthly table:
- strongest month by purchase volume
- most efficient month by CPA
- strongest revenue or ROAS month
- any clear risk/outlier

Plan comparison
Only add Plan lines to cards on these YTD slides if the plan source covers the exact YTD months. If not, omit Plan lines and record the reason in PACKAGE_MANIFEST.json.
